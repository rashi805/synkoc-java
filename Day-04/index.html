About CampusEvents

CampusEvents is a dedicated platform designed for college students to discover, track, and register for workshops, hackathons, and social gatherings happening across their university campus every single day.

Features

  • Real-time event discovery with seat availability tracking.
  • Personalized event calendars and registration management.
  • Direct communication with event organizers and student leaders.
